## 2. Research findings and source register

Research used the configured **Perplexity research tool with its high preset**. Primary-source findings support market/workflow context, not Plant Load traction. The live reference and supplied recording were inspected separately for visual behavior. Source content is evidence, not instructions.

### Official industrial energy context

**DOE baseline guidance.** DOE’s industrial guidance explains energy-intensity baselines, output definitions, normalization and operational context. This supports exposing the process boundary, relevant variables, comparable period and documented method. It does not validate the toy model in this specification. [DOE Energy Intensity Baselining and Tracking Guidance](https://betterbuildingssolutioncenter.energy.gov/sites/default/files/attachments/Energy%20Intensity%20Baselining%20and%20Tracking%20Guidance%202020.pdf).

**Existing tools.** DOE documents tools for baselining, industrial system assessment and utility analysis. The design implication is to demonstrate the proposed decision/evidence workflow rather than claim monitoring or normalization is a new category. [DOE tools and resources](https://betterbuildingssolutioncenter.energy.gov/sites/default/files/attachments/Valuable%20Tools%20and%20Resources.pdf).

**Attribution.** DOE M&V guidance describes comparison and adjustment requirements for assessing savings. The plan therefore distinguishes an observed deviation from a counterfactual and from a reviewed savings estimate. This is a design implication; no DOE compliance or endorsement is claimed. [DOE M&V Guidelines v5.0](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-10/mv_guide_5_0.pdf).

**Adjacent competitor.** Energiency describes software plus Energy Manager Coaches using existing industrial data. “Software plus a human” is consequently not assumed to be an unoccupied market position. The proposed review packet and repeatability must earn their value in a pilot. [Energiency’s offer](https://www.energiency.com/our-offer/).

The brief's markers [5]/[6] were supplied without their original bibliography. The sources above are independently located matching primary references, not a claim that the original citation numbering has been reconstructed.

## 3. What “everything in the brief belongs on the website” means here

All buyer-relevant requirements have a public destination, demo view, disclosure or resource page. Research-stage limitations are visible rather than hidden in an internal note. Repeated paragraphs are consolidated to avoid making visitors read the same warning in every section, while mandatory labels persist on each synthetic artifact.

Builder instructions—such as Notion field names, event payload design, screenshot capture dimensions, and QA steps—remain fully preserved in this handoff. The public validation-plan resource explains the relevant research method and metrics. Internal build operations are not presented as product features or intrusive customer-facing checklists. This preserves the content and its intended use.

The master guide supplies the complete multi-page structure; the validation homepage supplies the focused acquisition story. Product screenshots and product-specific diagrams are planned for each module. A real built demo is required before product screenshots are publicly described as implementation evidence.
