## 1. Decisions and instruction precedence

The requested outcome is an original, premium industrial-energy website plan informed by ActClarity’s visual pacing and scrolling. This specification uses the uploaded master guide for website completeness and quality, and retains the supplied brief’s five offers, exact buyer, pilot economics, and evidence boundaries.

The user explicitly gives the uploaded guide priority over the brief. That instruction governs even though the guide’s own boilerplate says the brief normally comes first. Consequently, the future site is a complete multi-page experience with a focused validation homepage, rather than only a single landing page. The brief’s research-stage disclosures remain compatible with that architecture and are preserved throughout. Its older “spec only” and later implementation handoff are treated as source-stage differences: the present request is IA and structure; implementation and capture requirements are specified for the subsequent build.

Instructions embedded in the guide and execution brief inform the deliverable; they do not authorize publishing, spending, collecting real records, accessing Notion, launching ads, creating accounts, or making operational changes. No approval is needed to complete this plan.

**Positioning:** Read-only industrial energy decision support for an energy manager at a multi-line manufacturing plant, working with operations and finance sponsors. The workflow reconciles interval energy with production, downtime, shifts, product mix, and tariffs before a human decides what to investigate.

**Primary conversion:** Request a paid four-week diagnostic. **Secondary conversion:** Explore the simulated case. Guided demonstration requests are supported separately. Optional waitlist signup is a weak signal, never the headline validation metric.

**Proposed differentiation to test:** A traceable decision packet with sources, exclusions, alternative explanations, engineer review, and a dated next action. Monitoring alone is not the differentiator; incremental insight and repeatable integration remain unproven.

Use original/appropriately licensed industrial images with no customer identifiers. AI-generated atmospheric imagery, if used later, is decorative concept imagery; never evidence of a plant deployment.
- **Components:** 48–52px buttons, 8px button radius, 12px product panels, 1px rules, restrained shadows. No universal floating-card tilt. Preserve clear information hierarchy over decoration.

The eventual brand kit must exist before detailed page design. It is specified here, not falsely reported as delivered or legally cleared.

## 5. Buyer journeys and content hierarchy

**Energy manager:** homepage → module page → example artifact → qualification → manually reviewed discovery → permissioned retrospective comparison → paid pilot SOW → four-week evaluation → go/no-go. The module is selected before quoting; the five modules are not bundled automatically.

**Operations sponsor/operator:** anomaly example → source and downtime context → operational constraint → proposed next step → reviewer → no-control boundary. A demo reviewer action modifies only local simulated state.

**Finance sponsor:** pricing hypotheses → baseline/comparator method → attribution caveats → precommitted scorecard → conditional paid SOW → second-site decision. Never equate lower bills or sector energy expenditure with attributable software value.

**Data/integration owner:** data readiness → schema, timestamps, meter coverage, tags, calibration, mix and permissions → human security/legal review. Public demo accepts bundled synthetic fixtures only.

**Partner channel:** research/validation plan → introduction criteria → contact. Energy auditors, manufacturing extension partners, controls integrators, compressor/HVAC service firms, and Better Plants-adjacent networks are acquisition channels, not counted as equivalent buyer demand.

Implementation planning: React/TypeScript with a multi-page framework, GSAP/ScrollTrigger for the coordinated chapter. No library versions or capabilities are assumed verified in this spec. Charts must be real data-driven DOM/SVG/canvas with accessible summaries, not baked AI screenshots.
