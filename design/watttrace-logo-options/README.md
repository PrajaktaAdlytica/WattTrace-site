# WattTrace — logo explorations

Three original image-generated concept boards. Exact brand spelling: **WattTrace**. These are raster exploration boards, not final vector masters or trademark-cleared identities.

## 01 — Trace Ribbon — recommended
An open, calligraphic W-shaped trace with thick-to-thin weight and an ascending terminal. Warm editorial serif wordmark. Best fit with the existing Mocha / Blueberry website and evidence-tracing story.

Motion direction: draw the path once, then resolve into the solid silhouette. Simplify the narrow central overlap in the eventual favicon master.

## 02 — Witness Seal
An organic sculptural emblem with a winding negative-space passage, paired with a humanist sans wordmark. Strongest compact silhouette and most suited to an application icon. The emblem is more abstract, so its meaning would be built through consistent use.

Motion direction: two source shapes converge gently, preserving the open passage. Avoid a perpetual spinning/loading animation.

## 03 — Quiet Current
An expressive WT ligature and bespoke-looking serif wordmark. Most editorial and formal. Refine the fine terminals before small-size use; the symbol needs a more robust micro version.

Motion direction: short sequential pen-stroke entrance. Keep the navbar version static.

## Research and rationale
Research used the configured Perplexity research tool with its high preset. The following primary agency case studies informed principles, not copied shapes:

- [Pentagram — EDP](https://www.pentagram.com/work/edp): a mark conceived through the motion of a point drawing a line.
- [Pentagram — Covariant](https://www.pentagram.com/work/covariant): a coherent curved visual behavior across identity and motion.
- [Pentagram — Univers](https://www.pentagram.com/work/univers): operational data and decarbonisation expressed through a broader identity system.
- [Pentagram — Urbint](https://www.pentagram.com/work/urbint): technical credibility with an accessible human tone.
- [COLLINS — Legence](https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/legence/): disciplined typography and engineered elegance.

Design judgement: a recognizable gesture and careful lettering serve WattTrace better than a generic lightning bolt, gear, circuit diagram or rigid badge. All three boards explore the existing latte, espresso and blueberry palette.

## Files and next production step
Each numbered PNG includes the main symbol/wordmark, standalone symbol, reversed lockup and an illustrative navbar application. Navbar images are generated applications, not screenshots of the built website. Full generation prompts are in prompts.json; generated using the built-in image_gen tool.

After selection, refine optical spacing and silhouette, construct vector masters, and prepare transparent light/dark lockups and favicon variants. Validate at 16, 24 and 32 pixels and in the actual navbar before replacement. No selected new symbol has been installed. Website text casing was corrected to WattTrace.
