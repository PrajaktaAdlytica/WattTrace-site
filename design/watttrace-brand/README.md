# WattTrace — Measured Record logo set

Selected recording-paper and stylus emblem, with a lighter Manrope Regular wordmark. The emblem was vector-traced from the selected concept and cleaned into filled paths. Lettering is outlined: SVGs have no font dependencies or embedded raster images.

## Included
- Horizontal logos, stacked logos, wordmarks and standalone symbols in espresso, blueberry, cream and black (16 SVG masters).
- Transparent high-resolution PNG horizontal logos in all four colors.
- Simplified optical favicon SVG and PNGs at 16, 32, 48, 64, 180, 192 and 512px; multi-resolution ICO.
- Contact-sheet preview and Manrope font license.

## Usage
Use espresso on latte and cream on blueberry/espresso. Keep clear space around the logo of at least half the wordmark capital height. Use the horizontal lockup at 150px or wider; use the optical favicon master for very small applications. Do not use the full detailed emblem at 16px. Preserve aspect ratio. The micro mark removes fine details while keeping paper roll, tracing line and descending stylus.

The website now uses the outlined horizontal logo in its header and footer, along with SVG/ICO favicon, Apple touch icon and web manifest icons. Brand casing is WattTrace. No trademark clearance claim is made.
