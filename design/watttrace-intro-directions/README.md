# WattTrace intro film — visual directions

Visual exploration only. No video rendered and no website changes made.

The three numbered boards match their display order in the conversation. Each has a keyframe and three shots from one proposed film.

## Reference review
Reviewed sampled opening frames of the supplied 88-second screen recording. The opening shows a full-width photographic film under navigation, an oversized brand name near the bottom, three discreet chapter labels and a supporting CTA. The main hero follows vertically. The aircraft and flower imagery is not carried into WattTrace.

## Shared production brief
- A roughly 10-second atmospheric intro above the existing homepage hero, not a loading screen or mandatory gate.
- Preserve the selected Measured Record emblem and light WattTrace wordmark.
- Use latte, espresso, blueberry and restrained copper.
- Keep navigation, copy and controls as accessible HTML overlays rather than burned into the video.
- Illustrative imagery only, with no claim of real plant access, telemetry or demonstrated savings.
- Muted playback; visible pause/play; immediate scroll or CTA access to the existing hero.
- Reduced motion and data-saving mode: a still poster by default, with optional deliberate playback. No forced camera movement or scroll scrubbing.
- Mobile: separately composed crop, stable poster while loading, readable title and control placement.
- The bottom storyboard strips are presentation notes; they are not website sections.

## 1 — The First Trace
A macro view of an industrial chart recorder. Strongest relationship to the approved logo.

0–3s: paper and mechanical stylus in warm sidelight. 3–7s: the stylus records one illustrative deviation. 7–10s: slow pullback reveals the preserved record; hold the final image. Avoid a jarring jump back to blank paper.

## 2 — The Working Plant
Architectural cinema inside an industrial hall. Strongest sense of real industrial scale.

0–3s: close metal junction. 3–7s: gentle movement through the pipe corridor. 7–10s: wide hall reveal as an illustrative copper trace fades. The generated board uses a serif display title; final production should use the approved lighter WattTrace wordmark.

## 3 — Anatomy of a Deviation
A sculptural compressor assembly reveals its interior beside a paper trace. Strongest match to the website's machine/data-layer story.

0–3s: intact housing. 3–7s: components separate along the shaft axis. 7–10s: hold the exploded assembly and illustrative trace. This is an explanatory visual metaphor, not an engineering model or observed machine action.
